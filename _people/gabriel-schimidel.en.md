## Introduction

Hello! My name is Gabriel Schimidel Ramos de Oliveira, I am 25 years old and I am currently studying Water Resources and Environmental Engineering at the Federal Fluminense University (UFF) 🎓. Since the first semesters of my undergraduate studies, I have developed a great interest in Data Science applied to the environment, with a special focus on climatology, hydrology, and numerical modeling, seeking to understand how statistical techniques and Artificial Intelligence can contribute to the environmental field 🤖🌱.

Currently, as a Scientific Initiation scholarship holder, I am developing research focused on predicting extreme wave events using machine learning 🌊💻. This project has given me a greater understanding of how machine learning models are used in the environmental field.

In my free time, surfing and the sea are my main passions, whether watching or practicing 🏄. In addition, I love spending my time at home, in the company of my dog ​​Caramelo 🐾 and my family. Trying to make the most of my free time by watching series and anime, playing video games, and studying topics of interest to me 📚.
